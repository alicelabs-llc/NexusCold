Authorized data and purpose

Use your own files or data you are authorized to process. Public availability does not remove data-subject rights or authorize unrestricted reuse. Document source, purpose and the appropriate basis for your activity.

Prohibited use

Do not use the product for phishing, impersonation, malware, fraud, harassment, doxxing, unlawful data sales or abusive messaging. Do not import third-party secrets, children’s data or sensitive categories without required safeguards and authorization. Do not bypass authentication, CAPTCHAs, blocks, quotas or source terms.

Checks and automation

Bound concurrency and volume; do not use DNS/SMTP for undue load or systematic mailbox enumeration. Respect policy responses, objections and provider limits. The verifier sends no DATA. Campaigns performed through another product must follow applicable rules, accurate sender identification and required opt-outs.

Agents and incidents

Automation operates under the configuring user’s authorization. Do not give models unnecessary keys, shell access or data. Privately report vulnerabilities to legal@alicelabs.site without accessing, copying or disclosing other people’s data to prove a flaw.
