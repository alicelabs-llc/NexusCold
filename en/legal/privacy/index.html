Who is responsible

AliceLabs LLC, identified as a Wyoming, United States entity in the product license, publishes TechDigital Prospect. Privacy questions and requests: legal@alicelabs.site. This notice covers this landing, its browser reviewer and the described application functions; it does not incorporate the activities of other linked stores or projects.

This browser reviewer

CSV files are read and analyzed in browser memory. Basic review does not transmit rows. If you enable enrichment, company names are queried in Wikidata (CC0 structured data) and GLEIF (LEI records) and domains in Google Public DNS for MX, SPF and DMARC. These providers receive the query and technical connection data. The full CSV is not uploaded and no AI model is used. If you connect your crawler, the selected site is sent to your configured HTTPS service with its access token; that service queries up to five public pages by default, respecting robots.txt. If Brave search is configured on that server, company name and city may be sent to that provider to discover websites. The token remains in tab memory and is not persisted in browser storage. Exports are generated by the browser. Clearing data removes the interface reference; closing or refreshing ends the session. This does not delete downloaded files, device copies or information retained by browser extensions.

The desktop application

Offline review runs locally. Enrichment sends necessary company names, locations and domains to selected sources. Mailbox checks query DNS and connect to MX servers to inspect recipient responses; they do not send a message body. Germán checkpoints are encrypted and saved with a separate key. Input and exported CSV files are not encrypted by this mechanism. The legacy workflow can retain an unencrypted contact cache; Germán jobs disable it.

Optional AI

Chat sends allowlisted aggregate metrics and your typed question to the selected endpoint. It does not automatically attach data rows or enriched web pages. Personal data you type into your question is transmitted. Local Ollama processes on the computer where it is installed; a remote endpoint processes on its configured server. The server operator determines its responsibilities and providers. An AliceLabs-managed service requires a separately agreed written scope and terms.

Web access, communications and providers

Hosting, DNS or CDN providers may process IP addresses, timestamps, requested resources and technical information to serve and protect the page. The page is served through GitHub Pages with its domain managed at Hostinger; this notice does not promise an unconfirmed physical location or log-retention period. GitHub serves the repository when you follow its links. Email links open your email client; we receive the information you choose to send for responses, licensing, support and applicable obligations. This site copy has no marketing form or checkout.
